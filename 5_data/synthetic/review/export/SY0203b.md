## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting and noted that the industrial designer was absent because of a supplier visit. She recapped the kickoff: a target age of four to eight, a selling price of 30 euros and a maximum production cost of 15 euros.
- The user interface designer reported that tablets for this age group come in seven, eight and ten inch sizes, with ten inch the most common. Because dimensions and camera positions vary, the team agreed to design for a single ten inch model.
- The marketing expert presented competitor research: kids' cases sell for 15 to 40 euros, mid-range cases are mostly EVA foam with a handle, and most look alike, so the product needs to stand out. Some expensive cases have a handle that folds into a stand.
- The user interface designer listed the core functions: drop protection, an easy-carry handle, open cutouts for buttons, camera and ports, and easy cleaning. The team agreed to include a carrying handle and dropped the shoulder strap over safety concerns.
- The handle-as-stand idea was left for the absent industrial designer to cost. The marketing expert took on the child safety regulations task, which the user interface designer had first offered to do.

**actions**
- The industrial designer, who was absent, will check whether a handle that folds into a stand fits within the 15 euro production cost.
- The industrial designer will finish the materials research (foam versus silicone) carried over from the kickoff.
- The marketing expert will check safety regulations for children's products, especially small parts such as hinges or clips. The user interface designer had first offered to do this.
- The user interface designer will prepare a layout of the cutouts and handle attachment for the ten inch model.
- The marketing expert will research trendy colours and styles for children aged four to eight.
- The project manager will send round the meeting notes and email the industrial designer his tasks.

**decisions**
- The case will be designed for one ten inch tablet model rather than several sizes.
- The case will include a carrying handle.
- The shoulder strap will not be included, because of safety concerns for young children and because the handle covers carrying.

**problems**
- Whether the handle can fold into a stand depends on the industrial designer's cost check against the 15 euro limit.
- The case material has still not been chosen, pending the industrial designer's research.
- A stylus holder was suggested but parked over small-part and loss concerns.
- The design needs to stand out from competitors, which mostly look alike.
