## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kick-off meeting, noted that the marketing expert was absent at a client meeting, and had the industrial designer and user interface designer introduce themselves.
- She presented the project: a voice-controlled kitchen timer that people can use while their hands are messy. It should sell for about 25 Euro, with production costs of at most 12.50 Euro per unit.
- The industrial designer warned that the budget is tight for good voice recognition and mentioned cheap offline chips with a fixed vocabulary. The user interface designer thought a fixed vocabulary might be enough for a timer.
- After a short whiteboard exercise, the team agreed that the timer should support several timers at once and should have one physical stop button as a backup to voice control.
- A wake word name and splash-proofing were raised but left open. The project manager handed out tasks, including market research for the absent marketing expert, and cancelled a cardboard mock-up the industrial designer had offered to build.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will research components (voice recognition chip, microphone, and performance with kitchen background noise) within the 12.50 Euro cost limit.
- The user interface designer will write up the command set and how the timer responds, plus a rough display sketch. The commands are the priority.
- The marketing expert (absent) was assigned market research by the project manager: what people currently use for timing in the kitchen, what they would want from a voice-controlled timer, and whether a wake word name helps.
- The project manager will email the marketing expert the brief and his task.

**decisions**
- The timer will support multiple simultaneous timers.
- The timer will be voice-first but will have at least one physical stop button as a backup.
- The cardboard mock-up will not be made, because the brief allows no physical prototypes until the conceptual design meeting.

**problems**
- The 12.50 Euro production cost limit may be tight for decent voice recognition.
- It is undecided whether voice recognition happens on the device or online.
- It is undecided whether the timer gets a wake word name such as 'Hey Chef'. This has been passed to marketing.
- It is open whether the timer needs to be waterproof or splash-proof. This will be revisited once the components are known.
