## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting and noted that the industrial designer was absent because of a training course, so his comparison of dock identification technologies was not yet available. The project manager confirmed that the minutes had been sent and reported that CityCycle has no dock hardware budget this year, treats stickers or tags on docks as maintenance, and does not want dock screens for now. The marketing expert presented the survey, which received 1,040 responses. The biggest complaint was typing the dock code, followed by full stations and broken bikes, and trip data showed an average of about forty seconds from arriving at a dock to unlocking. The user interface designer presented a two-tap unlock flow and a review of four other bike-share apps. The team agreed the version one functions, chose a guest mode in the app for tourists instead of a web page, and agreed what to pass on to the absent industrial designer.

**actions**
- The project manager will email the industrial designer this afternoon with the meeting's decisions and ask for the NFC and QR comparison by Monday.
- The industrial designer is to deliver the NFC and QR comparison by Monday, taking into account guest users and older phones.
- The marketing expert will send the phone model survey results to the industrial designer and the team tomorrow.
- The user interface designer will make wireframes of the core screens (map, unlock, return, report fault, guest mode) for the conceptual design meeting.
- The marketing expert will split the survey results into commuters and weekend riders.
- The project manager will get written confirmation from CityCycle that dock stickers or tags are covered by its maintenance budget.

**decisions**
- The unlock flow will be designed to work with either NFC or QR, and the technology will be chosen at the next meeting once the industrial designer's comparison is available.
- Version one will include unlocking, a station map showing bikes and free docks, return confirmation, fault reporting, and accounts and payment.
- Trip history is moved to a second version.
- Tourists will use a guest mode in the app and pay for a single ride with Apple Pay or Google Pay without an account. The QR-linked web page will not be built.
- The next meeting is the conceptual design meeting, at the same time next week.

**problems**
- The dock identification technology is still not chosen, pending the industrial designer's comparison.
- It is uncertain whether tourists will download an app for a single ride, and few tourists answered the survey.
- Some users have older phones that may not support NFC.
- CityCycle's coverage of dock stickers or tags under maintenance still needs written confirmation.
