## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting, welcomed back the industrial designer and recapped the functional design meeting: one ten inch tablet model, a carrying handle and no shoulder strap.
- The industrial designer reported that EVA foam costs about five euros per case against about nine for silicone and absorbs drops better, and the team chose EVA foam.
- He found that a handle folding into a stand would need a hinge and locking clip, adding about four and a half euros and pushing the cost to around sixteen euros. The marketing expert added that loose small parts like hinges and clips are flagged as choking hazards under the EN71 toy safety standard. The team therefore dropped the stand in favour of a one-piece handle moulded into the foam.
- The user interface designer presented the cutout layout for the ten inch model, with the handle along the top long edge so the case hangs landscape, and agreed to add raised bumper corners. The marketing expert presented bright two-tone colour schemes, which the team adopted, bringing the estimated production cost to roughly eleven to twelve euros.
- The mock-up for the detailed design meeting was first given to the user interface designer and then handed to the industrial designer, who will build it from real foam offcuts from the supplier.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will build a mock-up of the case from the supplier's foam offcuts for the detailed design meeting. This task was reassigned from the user interface designer.
- The industrial designer will prepare a final cost breakdown.
- The user interface designer will update the layout with raised bumper corners and send it to the industrial designer.
- The marketing expert will prepare three colour combinations to present.
- The project manager will send round the meeting notes.
- Checking how much packaging adds to the production cost was raised, but nobody took it on.

**decisions**
- The case body will be made of EVA foam rather than silicone.
- The handle will be moulded into the foam as one piece, with no hinge or clips.
- The folding handle stand is dropped, because it would exceed the 15 euro production cost and the hinge and clip would be choking hazards.
- The case will use a bright two-tone design, with a contrasting colour for the handle and corners.
- The corners will be raised like a bumper for drop protection.

**problems**
- Nobody is responsible for checking how much packaging adds to the production cost.
- Whether to put a logo on the case was deferred to the detailed design meeting.
- The cost estimate of eleven to twelve euros is rough and still needs a final breakdown.
