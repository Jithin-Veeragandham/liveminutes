## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting, noted that the industrial designer was absent through illness, and recapped the kickoff decision on a big, high-contrast display.
- The marketing expert presented the user panel's readability findings: digits at least 1.5 centimetres tall, black on white preferred, and most seniors preferring a belt or pocket clip to a wrist strap.
- The user interface designer proposed a single large button that cycles between steps today, the time and yesterday's steps, with an automatic reset at midnight. The team accepted this.
- Fall detection was dropped for cost and complexity reasons. A vibration goal alert was proposed and left pending panel feedback and costs. The battery question was handed to the absent industrial designer.
- The open task of checking component costs was offered by the user interface designer but taken by the marketing expert, who already has supplier price lists.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will check component costs: sensor, screen, button, clip and, if possible, a vibration motor.
- The marketing expert will ask the user panel whether seniors want a vibration alert when the daily goal is reached.
- The industrial designer (absent) will compare coin-cell and rechargeable batteries for size, cost and ease of changing within the clip housing.
- The industrial designer (absent) will check that the clip attachment is sturdy enough and within budget.
- The user interface designer will make a mock-up of the three screens and the single button with the large digits.
- The user interface designer will send the marketing expert information on screen sizes for pricing.
- The project manager will email the industrial designer with the decisions and his tasks, and will send round the minutes.

**decisions**
- The device will be worn on a clip (belt or pocket), pending the industrial designer's sturdiness and cost check.
- The display will be black on white, with step-count digits at least 1.5 centimetres tall.
- The device will have one large button that cycles between steps today, the time and yesterday's steps. Holding it down enters time-setting mode.
- The step count will reset automatically at midnight.
- Fall detection will not be included in this product.

**problems**
- Battery type (coin cell or rechargeable) is still undecided. Coin cells are disliked for being hard to change, and recharging is disliked by the panel.
- The vibration goal alert is undecided because of component cost and battery drain.
- Component costs are still unknown.
- The marketing expert still sees sales potential in a family app, but it was rejected for this version.
