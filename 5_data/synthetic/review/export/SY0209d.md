## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting, noted that the industrial designer was absent because he was at the supplier's factory, and confirmed that she had sent round the previous minutes.
- She reported the industrial designer's emailed figures: a name tag window would cost 0.95 Euros as a clear plastic insert or 0.70 Euros as a moulded foam pocket with clear film, and the revised supplier quote with the wedge back and second colour is 9.40 Euros, giving a total cost of 10.10 Euros, within the 12.50 Euro limit.
- The team chose the foam pocket because the insert could crack, and the user interface designer presented updated sketches showing the shallow wedge back and a landscape, business-card-sized name tag pocket centred on the back below the handle.
- The marketing expert presented six survey-based evaluation criteria scored from one (very good) to seven (bad), and the design scored mostly ones and twos, with a three for the lime green showing dirt.
- The toy safety check was first given to the user interface designer and later reassigned to the absent industrial designer, who will also be asked to get physical samples in both colours.

**actions**
- The industrial designer (absent) will check with the supplier whether the case counts as a toy under children's product safety rules. This was first assigned to the user interface designer and then reassigned to him.
- The industrial designer (absent) will get physical samples from the supplier, two in blue and two in lime green, for testing.
- The project manager will email the industrial designer about his tasks.
- The user interface designer will produce final drawings with the pocket dimensions for the supplier.
- The marketing expert will write up the evaluation scores as a section for the final report.
- The project manager will put together the final report and the minutes.

**decisions**
- The name tag window will be a moulded foam pocket with clear film (0.70 Euros) rather than a clear plastic insert.
- The name tag pocket will be centred on the back of the case below the handle, in landscape orientation, sized for a business-card-sized card.
- The selling price stays at 25 Euros.
- The total production cost is 10.10 Euros per case (9.40 Euros revised quote plus 0.70 Euros pocket), within the 12.50 Euro limit.

**problems**
- It is still unknown whether the case counts as a toy under children's product safety rules; the check now sits with the absent industrial designer.
- The lime green colour may show dirt and marks; this will be checked on the samples.
- Dirt may get in at the edge of the clear film on the name tag pocket.
- No physical sample has been tested yet, so the drop protection rating is not confirmed.
- The 25 Euro price is at the upper end of the competitor range and relies on the name tag and stand-up back to justify it.
- The industrial designer has not yet been told about his new tasks.
