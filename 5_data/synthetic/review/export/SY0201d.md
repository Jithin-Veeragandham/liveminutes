## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting, the last in the series, and noted that the marketing expert was absent at a regional sales meeting; she confirmed the minutes of the conceptual design meeting had been sent round.
- She reported the marketing expert's emailed user test results: all eight seniors from the walking group could read the step count without glasses and liked the ten-bar goal indicator, but three did not understand when to release the button during setup.
- The industrial designer presented a foam model of the dark grey, matte, pebble-shaped case (about four centimetres across and twelve millimetres thick) with the logo under the display, and reported that each extra strap colour adds about ten cents per unit; the team chose grey and navy straps.
- The user interface designer presented the final screens, lengthening the setup hold to three seconds and showing the word SET with a flashing goal; her proposal for a backlight was rejected on cost and battery grounds.
- The production cost now stands at 10.90 euros, leaving 1.60 euros for packaging and the leaflet; the packaging cost check was first given to the user interface designer and then moved to the industrial designer, and the absent marketing expert was given the leaflet and box text.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will get a quote for the packaging and the instruction leaflet from the case supplier by Friday. This task was first given to the user interface designer.
- The marketing expert will write the instruction leaflet text and the box wording.
- The project manager will email the marketing expert tonight about his task.
- The user interface designer will send the marketing expert the screen descriptions for the leaflet.
- The user interface designer will send the final screen specification to the industrial designer for the display supplier.
- The project manager will write up the minutes and the final report for management, including a note on fall detection for a later model.
- The industrial designer will photograph the foam model for the report.

**decisions**
- Straps will be offered in dark grey and navy blue, adding about ten cents per unit.
- The setup hold will be three seconds instead of two, and the display will show SET with the goal number flashing; each press adds 500 steps and holding again saves, with a default goal of 5,000 steps.
- The backlight is not included.
- Fall detection is excluded from this version of the product.
- The overall design is agreed: pebble-shaped dark grey matte case, twist-off battery cover, logo under the display, grey and navy straps.

**problems**
- The cost of packaging and the instruction leaflet is still unknown; if it exceeds the remaining 1.60 euros the 12.50 euro production cost limit will be broken.
- The marketing expert was absent, so the leaflet and box text task must be passed on to him by email.
