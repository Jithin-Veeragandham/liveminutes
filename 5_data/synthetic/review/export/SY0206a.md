## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting, and the team members introduced themselves and their roles.
- The project manager presented the project: a mobile app for the city bike-share docking stations that replaces the old card system. Management requires the app to let riders find a dock with free bikes, unlock a bike and pay for the ride. The development budget is 80,000 euros, with a launch planned for spring.
- In a first round of ideas, the marketing expert identified live dock availability as the riders' main need, and the user interface designer suggested the map could be the first screen.
- A reward scheme for returning bikes to emptier docks and a web version were raised but not pursued.
- The team agreed to build for both iPhone and Android, with find, unlock and pay as the core functions. Work for the functional design meeting was then divided among the team.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will run a short survey of regular riders on user requirements.
- The user interface designer will review competitor bike-share apps and capture screenshots of their main flows.
- The project manager will obtain the dock hardware specifications from the supplier Velodock. This task was first given to the industrial designer.
- The industrial designer will compare NFC, Bluetooth and QR codes as ways for the phone to communicate with the dock, including how durable QR codes are outdoors.
- The project manager will ask management whether payment is handled by an external provider.
- The project manager will send round the minutes of the meeting.

**decisions**
- The app will be developed for both iPhone and Android.
- The core functions are finding a dock, unlocking a bike and paying for the ride.
- The reward scheme for returning bikes to emptier docks is parked until budget allows.

**problems**
- It is unclear whether bike returns are handled only by the dock.
- Live availability depends on whether the docks are online, which is still unknown until the dock specifications arrive.
- It is unknown whether payment is designed in-house or handled by a provider.
- It is unknown whether the old cards will keep working, which matters to some older riders.
- The spring launch timeline is considered tight.
