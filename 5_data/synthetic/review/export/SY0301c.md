## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting and noted that the user interface designer was absent again, this time on a training course. She confirmed she had emailed him the minutes and his task from the functional design meeting, and said he had not yet sent his screen flow.
- The marketing expert reported the consortium's RFID list: seven of the twelve branches are fully tagged, three are partly tagged and two have no RFID. This confirmed the earlier decision to read both RFID tags and barcodes. He added that all library cards carry a barcode, so no separate card reader is needed.
- The industrial designer presented a full cost breakdown of 1,940 euros per unit before the casing. A brushed steel casing would bring the total to about 2,360 euros, and a moulded plastic casing to about 2,200 euros. She dropped her preference for steel, and the team chose moulded plastic and a freestanding pedestal.
- The marketing expert's statement that the idle screen would show library event adverts was not taken up, and the meeting moved on. The team provisionally favoured the consortium's green, pending the consortium's style guide.
- The project manager gave the absent user interface designer an extra task: mock-ups of the three screens. She also asked him to send his height and tilt range to the industrial designer.

**actions**
- The user interface designer, who was absent, will continue the screen flow for scanning and the receipt choice and the wheelchair height and tilt range, as assigned at the functional design meeting.
- The user interface designer, who was absent, was assigned by the project manager to produce mock-ups of the three screens on the 21-inch screen in the consortium green for the detailed design meeting.
- The user interface designer will send his height and tilt range to the industrial designer as soon as he has it, so that she can finalise the pedestal.
- The project manager will email the user interface designer about his new tasks.
- The marketing expert will ask the consortium whether it has a branding style guide.
- The industrial designer will get a proper quote for the plastic casing mould.
- The industrial designer will build a rough foam model of the pedestal for the detailed design meeting.

**decisions**
- Library cards will be scanned with the barcode scanner, so there will be no separate card reader.
- The kiosk will have a moulded plastic casing instead of brushed steel, giving an estimated cost of about 2,200 euros per unit.
- The kiosk will be a freestanding pedestal unit.

**problems**
- The colour is only provisionally the consortium green and depends on the consortium's style guide.
- The plastic casing cost of 260 euros is a catalogue figure and still needs a real quote.
- The pedestal and screen height cannot be finalised until the user interface designer supplies his height and tilt range, and his screen flow has not yet been delivered.
- Three branches are only partly RFID-tagged. The consortium expects tagging to be completed in about a year but would not commit to this.
