## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting, noted that the industrial designer was absent through illness, and welcomed the marketing expert, who had missed the kickoff and confirmed she had received the brief and minutes.
- He recapped the kickoff: the target age group of four to eight was agreed, while the material was still open after the industrial designer's EVA foam proposal. He reported that management requires the company logo and the company yellow on the case.
- The marketing expert presented her market research. Competitor cases sell for roughly 15 to 30 euros. Parents rank drop protection first, ease of cleaning second and a carrying handle third, and only one competitor case offered a stand.
- The user interface designer presented how children use the tablet. He recommended thick, grippy edges, soft covers over the volume buttons, a slightly recessed power button and an open charging port. He also proposed a raised lip around the screen instead of a separate screen protector.
- The team agreed on the raised lip and confirmed the handle that folds into a stand. The material question was handed to the absent industrial designer for costing. A parent survey was assigned to the marketing expert and then cancelled within the meeting.

**actions**
- The industrial designer, who was absent, will cost EVA foam against silicone relative to the 12.50 euro limit, check whether foam can be wiped clean, and send the figures before the next meeting. The project manager assigned this task.
- The project manager will email the industrial designer her task and the minutes today.
- The marketing expert will look into colour trends for children's products that work with the company yellow.
- The user interface designer will sketch the button cut-outs and the handle-stand for the conceptual design meeting.
- The project manager will send out the minutes.

**decisions**
- The case will have a raised lip around the screen, and no separate screen protector will be included.
- The handle that folds into a stand will be kept as a key feature and selling point.
- The planned parent survey through the company newsletter was cancelled, and the team will rely on the marketing expert's existing research.

**problems**
- The case material, EVA foam or silicone, is still unresolved and waits for the industrial designer's cost figures.
- The 12.50 euro production cost limit remains tight, especially if silicone is used.
- A shoulder strap was suggested but parked over safety concerns for young children.
- The raised lip protects against drops but not against scratches.
