## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting and the team introduced themselves. She presented the brief: a self-checkout kiosk for the Northfield library consortium's twelve branches, about forty units, with a production cost under 2,500 euros per unit.
- The team agreed that the kiosk will handle checkout only, because returns are already covered by the book drops and would need extra hardware.
- The marketing expert said the users range from children to older people, so the user interface designer wants large text, few steps (around three screens) and a screen height that works for wheelchair users. The industrial designer suggested a tiltable screen.
- The industrial designer declared that the kiosk would use RFID only. The marketing expert and the user interface designer raised concerns about untagged collections and cost, and the project manager deferred the question without a decision.
- The project manager first asked the marketing expert to arrange a branch site visit. She cancelled this once she remembered the client had supplied a staff and borrower survey, and asked him to analyse the survey instead.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will research the scanner options and other components and what they cost.
- The user interface designer will work on the screen flow and look at accessibility guidelines.
- The marketing expert will go through the client's survey, pull out the main user requirements and present them at the next meeting. He will also look for anything on RFID and on receipt preferences.
- The project manager will circulate the brief, the survey file and the minutes.

**decisions**
- The kiosk will support checkout only. Returns are excluded.

**problems**
- Whether the kiosk should read RFID tags, barcodes or both is unresolved. The team does not know which branches have RFID-tagged collections.
- It is unclear whether an RFID reader fits within the 2,500 euro per-unit budget, which the team considers tight.
- Whether receipts should be printed or emailed is open, pending the survey results.
