## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting and welcomed back the marketing expert, who confirmed he had received her email about the decisions of the previous meeting.
- The marketing expert presented his competitor research and user requirements: most competitors are clip-ons with small, hard-to-read screens priced between 20 and 40 euros, and seniors want readable numbers, one-handed fastening, no charging and a sense of progress. He reported that management requires the company logo to be visible on the device but leaves the colours open.
- The user interface designer presented her mock-ups, and the team added a ten-bar daily goal indicator with a default goal of 5,000 steps. The industrial designer priced the components at 10.80 euros per unit, including a 1.80 euro hypoallergenic silicone strap, which leaves 1.70 euros under the 12.50 euro limit before packaging.
- The team dropped the hourly vibration reminder and agreed on a dark grey, matte, pebble-shaped case with the logo under the display, a twist-off battery cover with a wide coin slot and a pin-and-loop strap clasp.
- Testing the mock-ups with seniors was offered by the user interface designer but taken on by the marketing expert, who has contacts with a seniors' walking group.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will test the user interface designer's mock-ups with a seniors' walking group before the next meeting. The user interface designer had offered to do this.
- The user interface designer will send the marketing expert full-size printouts of the screens for the test.
- The marketing expert will send the company logo file to the industrial designer today.
- The industrial designer will price straps in a couple of extra colours for the next meeting.
- The industrial designer will make a rough clay or foam model of the device for the detailed design meeting.
- The user interface designer will finalise the screens, including the goal bar and the setup sequence.
- The project manager will write up the minutes and send them round.
- Someone should check the cost of packaging and the instruction leaflet, but nobody took this on.

**decisions**
- A row of ten bars showing progress towards a daily goal will be added along the bottom of the display.
- The default daily goal will be 5,000 steps, set during setup by holding the button.
- The hourly vibration reminder is dropped.
- Fall detection remains parked.
- The battery will have a twist-off cover with a wide coin slot.
- The strap will use a pin-and-loop clasp rather than a buckle or a magnetic clasp.
- The case will be dark grey, matte and pebble-shaped, with the company logo small under the display.

**problems**
- The cost of packaging and the instruction leaflet is unknown and comes out of the same 12.50 euro budget, and nobody has taken on checking it.
- Whether to offer straps in several colours is undecided until extra colours have been priced.
- The mock-ups have not yet been tested with real seniors.
