## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting, introduced the team and presented the project: a wearable step counter for seniors aged about sixty-five and up, which should be original, trendy and above all easy to use.
- She explained that the work will run in three stages (functional, conceptual and detailed design), and the team practised using the whiteboard.
- She gave the financial targets: a selling price of 30 euros, a maximum production cost of 12.50 euros per unit, and a profit target of 5 million euros from international sales.
- The team agreed that the device needs a large, high-contrast display and only one or two buttons. They discussed whether it should be a clip-on or a wristband and deferred that choice to the next meeting.
- A fall detection feature was proposed but parked as probably out of budget. The project manager then assigned individual work for the next meeting, which is in thirty minutes.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will research competitor step counters for seniors. The user interface designer had offered to do this, but the marketing expert took it on as market research.
- The marketing expert will prepare the user requirements.
- The industrial designer will prepare the working design, including components and battery life, and will compare rechargeable and replaceable batteries.
- The user interface designer will prepare the technical functions design.
- Each team member will present a few slides on their part at the next meeting.
- The project manager will write up the minutes and send them round.
- The cost of hypoallergenic silicone straps was raised as something someone should check, but nobody took it on.

**decisions**
- The device will have a large, high-contrast display.
- The device will have minimal buttons, one or two at most.
- The choice between a clip-on and a wristband is deferred to the next meeting.
- Fall detection is parked for now because of the budget.

**problems**
- It is not yet decided whether the device will be a clip-on (more accurate for steps) or a wristband (harder to lose, easier to remember).
- The 12.50 euro production cost limit is tight for a good screen and rules out fall detection for now.
- Nobody owns checking the cost of hypoallergenic silicone straps.
- There is no branding guidance in the brief yet.
