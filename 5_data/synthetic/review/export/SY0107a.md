## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting, the team members introduced themselves, and the project manager presented the project: a voice-controlled kitchen timer that is original, trendy and user-friendly and can be operated hands-free while cooking.
- The team tried out the whiteboard by drawing their favourite kitchen gadgets.
- The project manager presented the budget: a selling price of 25 euros, a maximum production cost of 12.50 euros per unit, and a profit target of 50 million euros.
- In a first round of ideas, the team agreed the timer should support multiple simultaneous timers and use a wake word, and discussed kitchen noise, a backup button, a phone app and a fridge magnet without settling them.
- The project manager assigned individual preparation tasks for the functional design meeting, which follows in about thirty minutes.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will work on the working design, including what microphones are affordable.
- The user interface designer will work on the technical functions design.
- The marketing expert will work on user requirements, including research on what home cooks want from a kitchen timer.
- The project manager will write up and distribute the minutes.

**decisions**
- The timer will support more than one timer running at a time; the exact number is still to be determined.
- The timer will use a wake word.

**problems**
- Kitchen noise may stop the timer from hearing users, and a good microphone adds cost within the 12.50 euro limit.
- Whether to add a backup button for loud situations was left open.
- A phone app connection was parked as probably too expensive.
- A fridge magnet or stand was left for the conceptual design meeting.
- The number of simultaneous timers is undecided.
- The target group is broadly defined as home cooks.
