## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting, noted that the user interface designer was absent on a training course, and recapped the conceptual design meeting: an EVA foam body, a one-piece moulded handle with no stand, raised bumper corners and a bright two-tone design, with the logo question left for this meeting.
- The industrial designer presented a mock-up built from the supplier's foam offcuts using the user interface designer's updated layout with bumper corners. It was light, survived informal drop tests, and had a handle sized for a child's hand, but the camera cutout was about two millimetres too tight.
- The marketing expert presented three colour combinations, and the team chose a blue body with an orange handle and corners. The team agreed on a small logo embossed into the foam on the back, with nothing printed or stuck on.
- The industrial designer presented the final cost breakdown, corrected from 12.50 to 11.50 euros per unit excluding packaging, which is well under the 15 euro limit. A rough packaging estimate of 1 to 1.50 euros would bring the total to around 13 euros.
- The team evaluated the design against the brief and judged it original, reasonably trendy and user-friendly, and free of loose parts, though it still needs formal EN71 testing.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will get a packaging quote from the supplier at his meeting on Thursday. The marketing expert first offered to look into packaging.
- The user interface designer, who was absent, will widen the camera cutout and produce the final drawing for the production spec, including the logo position.
- The marketing expert will find out which test labs carry out EN71 testing and what it costs.
- The project manager will send round the meeting notes and email the user interface designer his task.

**decisions**
- The case will have a blue body with an orange handle and corners.
- The logo will be small and embossed into the foam on the back bottom corner as part of the mould. Nothing will be printed or stuck on.

**problems**
- The packaging cost is still only a rough estimate until the supplier's quote arrives.
- The camera cutout is about two millimetres too tight and must be fixed in the final drawing by the absent user interface designer.
- The case still needs formal EN71 safety testing, and the cost of that testing is unknown.
- A second colour version in purple and lime was suggested but not adopted for launch.
