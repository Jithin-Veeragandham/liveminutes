## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting and explained that the user interface designer was absent with the flu; the partner badge with the enlarged logo had been finished beforehand and was in the shared folder.
- The industrial designer reported the moulding company's quote of 1.54 euros per cup for the 300 ml polypropylene cup with in-mould label and ribbed grip band, at a minimum order of 10,000 units, plus a one-off mould cost of about 6,000 euros; the team chose the 10,000-unit order and rejected a smaller order of 5,000.
- With the higher cup cost a cafe loses fifty-four cents per unreturned cup, and the project manager reported that management will cover this gap, funded by a partner fee of five euros a month per cafe.
- The marketing expert presented the one-page staff guide, which the owner of Café Lindner approved with a bold line on refunds for cracked cups and a request to laminate it, and confirmed that he was happy with the slate grey colour.
- The final design was confirmed with an embossed 'return for one euro' on the base, and the team checked it against management's requirements, with only the fifty partner cafes still to be achieved.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will confirm the order of 10,000 cups with the moulding company and request a few pre-production samples with the embossing.
- The marketing expert will email the cafe owners from the trade fair list with the staff guide and the partner fee to recruit partner cafes.
- The marketing expert will have the staff guides laminated, one per cafe.
- The user interface designer (absent) is to prepare the print file for the partner badge once he has recovered.
- The project manager will send the minutes to the user interface designer, ask him to do the badge print file and tell him there is no rush.
- The project manager will write the final report for management and the minutes.

**decisions**
- The team will place the minimum order of 10,000 cups at 1.54 euros per cup; a smaller first order of 5,000 was rejected.
- The company will cover the cost of unreturned cups (fifty-four cents per cup), funded by a partner fee of five euros a month per cafe.
- The staff guide is final, with a bold line that cracked cups are still refunded, and will be laminated with one copy per cafe.
- The cup stays slate grey only; a limited launch edition in a second colour was rejected.
- The final cup is a 300 ml tapered, stackable polypropylene cup with a 90 mm rim, a moulded ribbed grip band, slate grey colour and an in-mould label logo.
- 'Return for one euro' will be embossed on the base of the cup at no extra cost per cup; adding the website address was rejected.
- The partner badge with the enlarged logo is considered basically final.

**problems**
- The target of fifty partner cafes in the first year has not yet been reached.
- The badge print file depends on the user interface designer, who is off sick.
- The pre-production samples with the embossing still have to be checked; lead time is about eight weeks.
