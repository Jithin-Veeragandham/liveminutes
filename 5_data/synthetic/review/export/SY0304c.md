## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting, noted that the industrial designer was off sick, and confirmed she had sent round the minutes of the previous meeting.
- She reported that the estates office would like the central sign near the main gate but has no separate budget for it, so the team left it out of this phase as a later idea.
- The user interface designer presented the sixty-centimetre layout with the car park name in white on a navy strip, the LED number below on a colour panel, and the agreed thresholds. The marketing expert reported that eleven visitors surveyed at the open day did not know the car park names and wanted a P symbol, and that most disliked flashing, which estates also discourage next to roads. The team therefore dropped flashing and cancelled the flashing mock-up.
- Relaying the industrial designer's email, the project manager reported that four of the five barriers have a spare mains supply but the underground library car park does not. She assigned the absent industrial designer to get an electrician's quote for a new supply there and to specify the mounting. The team chose a navy IP65 powder-coated aluminium casing and noted a cost of about three hundred and ninety pounds per sign, excluding the library supply.

**actions**
- The industrial designer (absent) is to get an electrician's quote for running a new mains supply at the library car park and to specify the mounting (post or wall bracket), checking the wall mounting with estates. The project manager assigned this.
- The project manager will email the industrial designer to tell him about his task.
- The project manager will ask the estates office whether they could pay for the library electrics separately.
- The user interface designer will produce the final layout with the P symbol, the navy strip and no flashing, shown on the navy casing.
- The marketing expert will write up the open day survey results and send them round.
- The project manager will send the minutes round.

**decisions**
- The central sign near the main gate is left out of this phase and flagged as a later idea.
- A P symbol will be added to the left of the car park name strip.
- The number will not flash. Red at five or fewer free spaces is the warning.
- The whole casing will be navy, matching the university signage.
- The barrier counters keep the nightly reset with attendant correction for now.

**problems**
- There is no spare mains supply at the underground library car park, and the cost of a new supply is unknown. It could push that sign over the four-hundred-pound budget.
- At about three hundred and ninety pounds per sign, only about ten pounds of margin is left.
- Barrier counter drift is still only partly addressed by the nightly reset.
- The marketing expert's suggestion of a QR code on the casing for a future app was not agreed and was left open.
- The visitor survey sample is still small, with eleven open day visitors plus two earlier respondents.
