## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting, noted that the marketing expert was absent because she was meeting a retail buyer, and said she had emailed her colour options. He recapped the conceptual design meeting: closed-cell EVA foam, the two-position stand and the embossed logo had been agreed, while the colour had been parked.
- The industrial designer reported that the supplier's foam prototype had arrived, that the two-position hinge does not change the handle cost, and that a two-colour moulding run adds 0.90 euros per case. The user interface designer reported that his six-year-old niece managed the upright watching position easily but found the low drawing position too stiff. The angles were fine. The industrial designer offered to have the drawing notch made shallower.
- The industrial designer declared that an anti-bite coating would be added to the handle and edges, but the user interface designer raised safety and grip concerns and the project manager set it aside without a decision. The user interface designer presented the final button layout, which was signed off.
- The estimated production cost came to 10.20 euros: 8.20 euros base, 1.10 euros tooling share and 0.90 euros for the two-colour run. This is against the 12.50 euro limit and a 25 euro selling price. The team then chose a yellow body with a teal handle and edges. The packaging copy was assigned to the absent marketing expert, and the product was evaluated against the kickoff goals.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will ask the supplier to make the drawing-position notch shallower so that it needs less force.
- The user interface designer will test the revised handle with his niece when it arrives.
- The marketing expert, who was absent, will write the packaging copy and selling points (drop protection, wipe-clean, handle-stand, ages four to eight). The project manager assigned this task.
- The project manager will email the marketing expert her packaging task tonight.
- The industrial designer will send photos of the prototype to the marketing expert for the packaging.
- The industrial designer will ask the supplier for a safety data sheet on the anti-bite coating.
- The project manager will send the minutes and the final report to management.

**decisions**
- The case will be two-colour, with a yellow body and a teal handle and edges, at an extra 0.90 euros per case.
- The drawing-position notch of the handle will be made shallower to reduce the push force.
- The final button layout was signed off: one moulded soft membrane over the volume buttons on the left edge, a power button on top recessed about two millimetres, and a fully open charging port on the bottom.

**problems**
- Whether to add the anti-bite coating (about 0.60 euros per case) is unresolved. Its safety for chewing children and its effect on grip are in question.
- The foam dents when children chew it.
- The push force of the revised drawing notch is untested until the new part arrives.
