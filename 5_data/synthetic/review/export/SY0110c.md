## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting and confirmed that the minutes and the marketing expert's trade fair notes had been sent round.
- The industrial designer reported on the week-long dishwasher test at Café Lindner, which the marketing expert had arranged: twelve polypropylene samples went through about 140 cycles with no warping or cracks and the lids still fit, but the pad-printed test logo faded badly.
- The team confirmed polypropylene and chose in-mould labelling for the logo, which brings the cup to about 1.35 euros, and added a moulded ribbed grip band instead of a cardboard sleeve.
- The team settled on a single 300 ml size with the 90 mm rim for launch and a single slate grey colour for all cafes, rejecting cafe-specific colours.
- The user interface designer presented the counter return flow, including a swap option for customers buying another drink, and the slate grey partner badge, both of which were accepted.
- With the higher cup cost, a cafe now loses about thirty-five cents per unreturned cup, and the project manager will take the question of who covers this to management.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will get a quote from the moulding company for the 300 ml cup with in-mould labelling and the ribbed band.
- The user interface designer will enlarge the logo on the partner badge.
- The marketing expert will write a one-page staff guide on the refund and swap process and run it past the owner of Café Lindner; the user interface designer had first offered to do this.
- The marketing expert will check the slate grey colour with the owner of Café Lindner.
- The project manager will raise the cost of unreturned cups with management.
- The project manager will write the minutes.

**decisions**
- Polypropylene is confirmed as the cup material after the Café Lindner dishwasher test.
- The logo will be applied by in-mould labelling instead of pad printing, at about fifteen cents extra per cup.
- The cup will have a moulded ribbed grip band so no cardboard sleeve is needed, and it stays stackable.
- There will be one size of 300 ml with the 90 mm rim for launch; a large size may be looked at later.
- All cups will be one neutral slate grey colour, the same for every cafe.
- At the counter, staff either refund the one-euro deposit or swap a used cup for a clean one when the customer buys another drink; cracked cups are still refunded.
- The partner badge will be a round slate grey window sticker with the cup outline and the same logo as on the cup.

**problems**
- Cafes now lose about thirty-five cents per unreturned cup; whether the company will cover this (for example through a partner fee) is a management decision still pending.
- Whether to offer a large size later is still open.
- The actual cost of the in-mould label and ribbed band still has to be confirmed by the manufacturer's quote, and whether more samples are needed depends on it.
