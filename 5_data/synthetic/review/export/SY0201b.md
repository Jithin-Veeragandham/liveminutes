## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting and noted that the marketing expert was absent through illness, so his user requirements and competitor research could not be presented. She confirmed that the kickoff minutes had been sent round.
- The industrial designer presented the working design. He reported that a three-axis accelerometer makes a wristband accurate enough for step counting, and that a replaceable coin cell with a segment display would last about a year. A rechargeable battery would add about 1.50 euros and need regular charging.
- The user interface designer presented the technical functions. She proposed that the device show today's steps by default, with a single button that cycles to yesterday's steps and distance, and with setup done by holding the button down.
- The team chose a wristband, a replaceable coin cell battery, a high-contrast segment display and a single button. An hourly vibration reminder was left open because of its battery cost, and fall detection stayed parked.
- The task of checking strap costs was first given to the user interface designer and later moved to the industrial designer. Tasks for the absent marketing expert will be passed on to him by email.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will check the cost of soft hypoallergenic silicone wrist straps. This task was first given to the user interface designer.
- The user interface designer will prepare mock-ups of the interface, showing the display layout and the button behaviour, for the conceptual design meeting.
- The industrial designer will price the components, in particular the display and the case.
- The project manager will email the marketing expert today with the decisions and his tasks.
- The marketing expert will finish the competitor research, including screens, and send it round before the next meeting.
- The marketing expert will send his user requirements before the next meeting.
- The marketing expert will ask management whether there is branding guidance, such as a logo or colours that must be used.

**decisions**
- The device will be a wristband rather than a clip-on.
- The device will use a replaceable coin cell battery rather than a rechargeable one.
- The device will use a large, high-contrast segment display rather than a colour screen.
- The device will have a single button. By default it shows today's steps, and pressing the button cycles to yesterday's steps and then distance.
- Fall detection remains parked because of the budget.

**problems**
- The marketing expert's user requirements and competitor research were not available, so decisions were made without them.
- The cost of the display, and therefore whether the 12.50 euro production cost can be met, is not yet known.
- The cost of hypoallergenic silicone straps is still unknown.
- Whether to include an hourly vibration reminder is undecided, because it would cut battery life to a few months.
- There is still no branding guidance.
