## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting, noted that the industrial designer was absent because he was at the casing supplier for the seal test, and welcomed back the user interface designer after her illness. She recapped the decisions from the conceptual design meeting and presented the industrial designer's foam model and his cost sheet, which still totals 12.20 Euro. The user interface designer presented the updated interaction flow with commands starting with 'Tico', spoken confirmations, a fallback name for unlisted timers and a three-row display, and confirmed that everything fits in about 170 of the 200 words. The marketing expert presented four evaluation criteria scored on a seven-point scale, and the team rated the design 2 for voice reliability, 1 for the button, 2 for looks and 3 for price. The team chose a white body with an orange stop button. The project manager assigned the absent industrial designer the task of confirming the two-colour casing cost, and cancelled a handling test the marketing expert had offered, because the design must be signed off at this meeting.

**actions**
- The industrial designer (absent) will get the casing supplier to confirm the two-colour casing (white body, orange button) and send the final cost, to confirm it stays under 12.50 Euro. This was assigned by the project manager.
- The project manager will email the industrial designer his task tonight.
- The user interface designer will make a quick-start card for the box listing the main commands.
- The project manager will write up the final report and minutes.

**decisions**
- The timer will have a white body with an orange stop button.
- The interaction flow is accepted as presented: commands start with 'Tico', the timer repeats the timer name and time as confirmation, and the display shows three rows (name and countdown) with the ringing timer flashing.
- Timers with names not on the vocabulary list will fall back to 'timer one', 'timer two' and so on.
- The final design was evaluated on four criteria (scale 1 to 7, 1 best): voice reliability 2, button ease of use 1, looks 2, price 3.

**problems**
- The production cost of 12.20 Euro leaves only 30 cents of margin, and the orange button may add a few cents. The exact amount is not yet confirmed by the supplier.
- The result of the seal test at the casing supplier was not yet available.
