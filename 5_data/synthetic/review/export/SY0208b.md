## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager welcomed back the industrial designer, recapped the kickoff decisions and confirmed that he had received her email about his tasks.
- The marketing expert reported that existing self-checkout kiosks cost between 9,000 and 20,000 euros per unit, and that users most often complain about missed items in stacks, confusing error messages and security tags that are not deactivated. She also reported that users want a running list of their borrowed items on screen.
- The industrial designer presented component costs: about 600 euros for an RFID pad, 150 euros for a barcode scanner, 700 for the touchscreen, 500 for the PC, 800 for the casing and 200 for a thermal printer, about 2,950 euros in total. The team agreed to support both RFID and barcodes and to keep the printer.
- The user interface designer reported about 1,200 checkouts a day at the main branch, with peaks on Saturday mornings and weekdays from three to six. He set a target of under one minute per borrow and presented a flow that starts with a borrow/return screen with language names.
- Voice instructions were proposed and parked, and the question of connecting to the library catalogue system was raised but not assigned to anyone.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will look into how the kiosk deactivates security tags for RFID and barcode items. The marketing expert offered first, but he took it because it is tied to the reader.
- The industrial designer will firm up the component costing and start on the casing.
- The user interface designer will turn the user flow into rough screen sketches for borrowing and returns.
- The marketing expert will research accessibility guidelines, such as screen height for wheelchair users and children.
- The project manager will send the minutes out tonight.
- Checking which catalogue systems libraries run and whether there is a standard connection was raised, but nobody took it.

**decisions**
- The kiosk will read both RFID tags and barcodes.
- A small thermal receipt printer will be included instead of email-only receipts.
- The start screen will have two large buttons, borrow and return, with language names along the bottom.

**problems**
- Integration with the libraries' catalogue systems is unresolved and has no owner.
- Casing costs could rise and eat into the roughly 550 euro margin under the 3,500 euro target.
- Security tag deactivation must be solved to avoid gate alarms, a common complaint about existing kiosks.
- Borrowing must be fast, under a minute, to handle after-school and Saturday peaks.
- Voice instructions were parked over noise concerns in a quiet library.
