## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting, noted that the industrial designer was absent at a supplier visit, and had the team introduce themselves.
- She presented the brief from the university estates office: a parking availability display at each car park entrance showing free spaces, with an optional central sign near the main gate, a budget of about four hundred pounds per unit, and installation by the start of next term.
- The marketing expert described the main user problem as drivers circling for spaces, and the team agreed that the target users are staff, students and visitors, and that the display should show the number of free spaces with a colour-coded background.
- The marketing expert stated that the signs would be solar powered, but nobody agreed and the point was left unresolved.
- The project manager assigned the absent industrial designer to research sensing options and outdoor casing, and the team agreed to limit the scope to physical signs for now, with an app flagged as a possible later idea.

**actions**
- The industrial designer (absent) is to compare barrier counters and per-bay sensors with rough per-unit costs, and to look at whether the casing can survive outdoors. The project manager assigned this.
- The project manager will email the industrial designer about his task and send the minutes and the brief to the team.
- The marketing expert will survey about twenty to thirty campus users, students and staff, and try to include a few visitors.
- The user interface designer will sketch several screen layouts showing the number with a colour background, in a couple of screen sizes.

**decisions**
- The target users are staff, students and visitors.
- The display will show the number of free spaces with a green, amber or red colour behind it.
- The scope is limited to physical signs for now. An app is flagged as a possible later idea.

**problems**
- The power source is unresolved. Solar power was put forward as settled, but nobody agreed, and the user interface designer started to raise a concern about the underground car parks under the library.
- The sensing method is undecided: barrier counters are cheaper but may drift when cars tailgate. This is waiting on the industrial designer's research.
- Screen size is unknown and depends on the industrial designer.
- The budget of four hundred pounds per unit is tight for an outdoor screen.
- It is undecided whether the central sign near the main gate will be included.
