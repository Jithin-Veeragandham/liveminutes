## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting and noted that the marketing expert was absent due to illness. He reported that the Velodock docks have an NFC reader, a small screen and a 4G connection, so live dock availability is possible, and that the dock handles bike returns by itself. He also reported that payment will be handled by an external provider under an existing contract. The user interface designer presented four competitor apps, all of which open on a map, and the team agreed that the map will be the app's first screen. The industrial designer compared NFC, Bluetooth and QR codes and advised against printed QR stickers outdoors. The team agreed on NFC for unlocking, with a QR code shown on the dock's screen as a fallback if the screen supports it.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will finish the rider survey and present it at the next meeting.
- The marketing expert will add a question to the survey on whether riders, especially older riders, still need the old cards.
- The project manager will message the marketing expert about the survey question.
- The project manager will ask Velodock whether the dock screen can display a QR code.
- The user interface designer will make rough mockups of the find, unlock and pay screens.
- The user interface designer will put the competitor screenshots in the shared folder.
- The industrial designer will look into how the NFC tap works with the dock's reader model.
- The project manager will forward the Velodock spec sheet to the industrial designer.
- The project manager will send round the minutes.
- Checking the payment provider's per-transaction fees was mentioned, but nobody took this task.

**decisions**
- The map will be the first screen of the app.
- Bikes will be unlocked by NFC, with a QR code shown on the dock screen as a fallback if the screen can display one.
- The app will have no return button, because the dock handles returns and the app is notified when the ride ends.
- The core functions are mapped as follows: find is the map, unlock is NFC, and pay goes through the external provider.

**problems**
- It is unknown whether the basic dock screen can display a QR code.
- It is still unknown whether the old cards will keep working, which matters to older riders.
- The payment provider's per-transaction fees are unknown, and they could add up for short rides.
- The rider survey is not finished, with about twenty responses so far.
- Some cheap or older phones may lack NFC.
