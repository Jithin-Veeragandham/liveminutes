## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting, noted that the user interface designer was absent through illness, and confirmed she had circulated the brief, survey file and minutes from the kickoff.
- The marketing expert presented the survey results. Borrowers mainly want speed, independence from staff and readable screens. About sixty percent prefer emailed receipts, but most respondents over sixty-five want a printed slip. Staff comments show RFID tagging is patchy across branches, and the survey has no clear list of tagged branches.
- The industrial designer presented component costs, about 2,100 euros per unit with everything included. She withdrew her earlier RFID-only position, and the team agreed the kiosk will read both RFID tags and barcodes. The team also agreed that users choose email, print or both on the final screen.
- The marketing expert's suggestion of fine payment at the kiosk was set aside as out of scope. The project manager assigned the absent user interface designer the scanning and receipt screen flow and the wheelchair height and tilt work.

**actions**
- The user interface designer, who was absent, was given a task by the project manager. He will work out the screen flow for the scanning screen and the receipt choice so that it supports both RFID and barcode within three screens. He will also define the screen height and tilt range for wheelchair users.
- The marketing expert will contact the consortium for a list of which branches have RFID-tagged collections.
- The industrial designer will prepare a detailed cost breakdown with both scanners and the printer, including a closer look at the casing, for the conceptual design meeting.
- The project manager will email the user interface designer the minutes and his task.

**decisions**
- The kiosk will read both RFID tags and barcodes.
- Receipts will be offered by email, print or both, chosen on the final screen, and a thermal printer will be included.

**problems**
- It is still unknown which branches have fully RFID-tagged collections.
- The estimated cost of about 2,100 euros per unit leaves little margin under the 2,500 euro limit, and the casing cost is uncertain.
