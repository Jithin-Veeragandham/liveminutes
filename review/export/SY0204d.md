## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting, noted that the user interface designer was absent on a training course, and confirmed that the previous minutes had gone round.
- The marketing expert reported that eleven of twelve user panel members could read the 1.8 centimetre digits on the mock-up at arm's length, but that some wanted bolder digits and a larger star icon.
- She gave an updated parts cost of 10.60 euros with the coin cell and steel clip. The open task of finding assembly and packaging costs was offered by the industrial designer but taken by the marketing expert, who already has the relevant supplier contacts.
- The industrial designer presented a foam model of the housing with the spring-steel clip, the twist-off battery cap and a moulded safety cord loop. The team kept the loop and chose a single dark blue housing colour.
- The team reviewed the user interface designer's screen layout and asked for the word 'yesterday' to be written out. The marketing expert raised the family app again and it was rejected. In the evaluation the product met the simplicity and readability aims, with total cost still to be confirmed.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will get quotes for assembly and packaging costs, which the industrial designer had first offered to do, and will report at once if the figure comes close to the 1.90 euro margin.
- The user interface designer (absent) will update the screen layout with bolder digits at the same height, a larger star icon and 'yesterday' written out instead of a Y label.
- The user interface designer (absent) will write a short instruction card on changing the battery and setting the time.
- The industrial designer will make a final printed plastic model in dark blue, including the safety cord loop, for sign-off.
- The project manager will email the user interface designer with the changes and her tasks, and will send round the minutes.

**decisions**
- The step-count digits stay 1.8 centimetres tall but will use a bolder, heavier font.
- The star icon will be made larger, about half the height of the digits.
- The safety cord loop moulded into the top of the clip will be kept. No cord will be included in the box.
- The housing will come in one colour only for launch: dark blue with a white screen face.
- The yesterday screen will show the word 'yesterday' written out instead of a Y label.
- The family app is not part of this product.

**problems**
- Assembly and packaging costs are still unknown. Parts cost 10.60 euros, which leaves 1.90 euros under the 12.50 euro limit.
- The marketing expert still sees demand for a family app, but it has been rejected for this version.
- The marketing expert would have liked several housing colours, but only one colour will be made for launch.
