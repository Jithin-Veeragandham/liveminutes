## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting, introduced herself, and the industrial designer and user interface designer introduced themselves. The marketing expert was absent because she was attending a hospitality trade fair in Cologne, and the team saw this as a chance to gather input from cafe owners there.
- The project manager presented the project: a reusable coffee cup with a deposit system, sold to cafes, where end users pay a deposit and return the cup to any partner cafe to get it back.
- Management's constraints are a deposit of one euro per cup, a production cost under three euros per cup, and a target of fifty partner cafes in the first year.
- The industrial designer raised durability in hot industrial dishwashers and suggested a collapsible silicone cup, while the user interface designer raised the question of how cups are returned and suggested a tracking app with a QR code; neither idea was adopted.
- The team agreed that the cup must be dishwasher-safe and fit standard takeaway lid sizes, and distributed research tasks ahead of the functional design meeting.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will research durable, dishwasher-safe materials within the three-euro production cost and bring options to the next meeting.
- The user interface designer will investigate the return process and how existing deposit systems handle it.
- The marketing expert (absent) is to talk to cafe owners at the trade fair about whether they would join a deposit system and what they would want from it.
- The project manager will write up the minutes and send them to the marketing expert together with her task.

**decisions**
- The cup must be dishwasher-safe.
- The cup must fit the standard takeaway lid sizes cafes already use.
- The tracking app idea is on hold until the marketing expert's findings are available.

**problems**
- With a one-euro deposit and a production cost of up to three euros, lost cups could be costly for cafes; the team left open how to address this.
- Whether a collapsible silicone design is viable remains open, given cleaning concerns.
- The return mechanism (counter, machine or app) has not been decided.
