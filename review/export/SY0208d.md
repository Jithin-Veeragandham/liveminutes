## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the detailed design meeting with all four members present, confirmed that the user interface designer had received her email and redone his sketches for landscape, and recapped the conceptual design decisions.
- The industrial designer presented a to-scale foam mock-up. It has a screen tilted about twenty degrees at ninety centimetres, a combined RFID and desensitiser shelf, the barcode scanner and printer slot on the right, and about seventy centimetres of knee space. The mock-up showed that the PC box needs a ventilation fan, which raises the cost to about 3,390 euros, 110 euros under target.
- He reported that two of three libraries support SIP2 and the third uses a newer web API, so the team adopted SIP2 as the standard. The marketing expert reported that two libraries want the swappable front panel (dark blue and green) as an option, so the team made it an optional add-on.
- The user interface designer presented the landscape flow with a running item list, a count-check screen with a one-at-a-time fallback, and plain-language error messages. His headphone-jack idea for audio instructions was left out, and he took over a user test with older borrowers that the marketing expert had offered to run.
- Running costs such as paper rolls and servicing were raised, but nobody took the task. The team evaluated the design as fast, accessible and under budget, though with a tight margin.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will email the catalogue system vendor of the third library about whether a SIP2 bridge exists.
- The user interface designer will run a user test with older borrowers and time it against the one-minute target. The marketing expert offered first, but he took it because the screens are his.
- The marketing expert will give the user interface designer a contact at the central library for the test.
- The marketing expert will prepare the pitch for the library authorities, including the panel colours.
- The industrial designer will bring the foam mock-up to the pitch.
- The project manager will write the final report and send the minutes tonight.
- Checking the running costs (paper rolls, servicing) was raised, but nobody took it.

**decisions**
- A small ventilation fan (about 40 euros) will be added for the PC, bringing the unit cost to about 3,390 euros.
- SIP2 will be the standard connection to library catalogue systems.
- The grey body is standard, and the colour front panel will be an optional add-on.
- Audio instructions via a headphone jack will be left out of this version.
- The team evaluated the kiosk as meeting the speed, accessibility and budget criteria.

**problems**
- One of the three libraries uses a newer system with a web API instead of SIP2, so a bridge or second connector may be needed.
- The cost margin is only about 110 euros under the 3,500 euro target.
- Running costs such as paper rolls and servicing have not been estimated, and nobody owns this task.
