## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting and the marketing expert and user interface designer introduced themselves; the industrial designer was absent because of a long-planned supplier visit to a moulding plant in Rotterdam.
- The project manager introduced the project: a reusable coffee cup with a deposit system for a client chain of about forty coffee shops at train stations and on campuses, aimed at commuters and students, with a production cost under three euros per cup and the client's logo in dark green and white.
- The team agreed that a lid is essential and must be included within the three-euro cost.
- The marketing expert identified the deposit amount as the key issue, since too low a deposit discourages returns and too high a deposit discourages uptake. The user interface designer raised a QR code and app or a physical token as ways to prove a deposit was paid, and the marketing expert doubted that commuters want another app.
- The team decided not to choose materials without the industrial designer's input, and the project manager will send him his tasks after the meeting.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will research deposit amounts and return rates in existing deposit cup schemes.
- The user interface designer will explore options for the return mechanism, such as a QR code with an app or a token, and present them at the next meeting.
- The industrial designer will look into materials and durability, including how many uses a cup can last and whether the lid can be made of the same material.
- The industrial designer will check which dishwasher temperatures the cup must withstand. This task was first given to the user interface designer and then moved to the industrial designer.
- The project manager will email the industrial designer the slides and action list.
- The project manager will ask the client whether unreturned cups are a problem for them.
- The project manager will put the client's corporate colours in the shared folder.

**decisions**
- The lid is part of the product and must fit within the production cost of under three euros per cup.
- Material choices will not be made until the industrial designer has given his input.
- The dishwasher temperature check was moved from the user interface designer to the industrial designer.

**problems**
- The deposit amount is still open, pending the marketing expert's research.
- It is undecided whether returns will use an app with QR codes, a physical token, or another method, and there is concern that commuters will not want another app.
- It is unclear whether the client minds cups not being returned, given that the café keeps the deposit.
- The material for the cup and lid is not yet known and depends on the industrial designer's research.
