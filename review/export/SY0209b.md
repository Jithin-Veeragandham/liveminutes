## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the functional design meeting and noted that the marketing expert was absent through illness. She confirmed she had sent round the kickoff minutes and reported early survey feedback from the marketing expert, which pointed to drop protection and easy cleaning as parents' main concerns. The industrial designer presented his drop test research, and the team set a drop rating target of 1.2 metres; he then compared silicone, polycarbonate and EVA foam, and the team chose EVA foam for the body. The user interface designer presented the functional design, covering a carrying handle, covered power and volume buttons, an open charging port, a camera cut-out and a raised lip around the screen. The team rejected the fold-out stand because of the finger-pinching risk and chose a fixed moulded handle. The team also dropped swappable covers, and the task of checking competitor prices moved from the user interface designer to the absent marketing expert.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will send the full survey results to the team before the next meeting.
- The marketing expert will check the retail prices of existing children's tablet cases. This task was first given to the user interface designer and then reassigned.
- The user interface designer will sketch the fixed handle and the positions of the cut-outs.
- The industrial designer will get a supplier quote for the whole EVA foam case, including the handle and the raised lip.
- The project manager will write up and send round the minutes.
- The project manager will email the marketing expert about his tasks and about the decision against the fold-out stand.
- The industrial designer's task of costing a hinge for a fold-out stand was dropped once the stand was rejected.

**decisions**
- The case should survive a drop of 1.2 metres.
- The case body will be made of EVA foam with thick corners.
- The power and volume buttons will be covered by press-through foam bumps, the charging port will be an open cut-out, and there will be a cut-out for the rear camera.
- The screen will be protected by a raised lip rather than a flap.
- The case will have a fixed handle moulded into the foam, with no fold-out stand.
- Swappable covers are dropped because of the budget.

**problems**
- Only about a dozen survey responses have come in so far.
- It is still unknown whether 25 Euros is a competitive price. The price check now sits with the absent marketing expert, and it is unclear whether he can finish it before the next meeting.
- Whether to give the back of the case a wedge shape so it can stand up was deferred to the conceptual design meeting, because of concerns about extra foam and weight.
- The name tag window is still undecided and waits on teacher feedback from the survey.
- Colours were not discussed and were deferred to the next meeting.
- The marketing expert has not yet been told that his fold-out stand idea was rejected.
