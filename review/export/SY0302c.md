## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting, noted that the user interface designer was absent because she was ill, and recapped the decisions from the functional design meeting.
- The industrial designer presented his cost breakdown. The fifty-word chip is too small, and the two-hundred-word chip costs 5.20 Euro. The full design comes to 12.20 Euro with a splash-proof casing and 13.80 Euro with a fully waterproof one.
- The team agreed on the larger chip, a splash-proof casing, two microphones (which performed much better than one in the noise test) and AA battery power.
- The marketing expert reported on his wake word test. 'Pepper' caused false triggers, 'Hey Chef' was still mixed, and 'Tico' was preferred by eight of twelve participants, so the team chose 'Tico'. He also reported that users want a rounded, attractive look.
- Because the user interface designer was absent, the project manager assigned her the task of updating the interaction flow and display, and said she would email it to her. The industrial designer was asked to build the foam model he had first offered at the kick-off meeting.

**actions**
- The user interface designer (absent) will update the interaction flow so commands start with 'Tico', design the display layout for three named timers, and add the spoken confirmations, checking that they fit the two-hundred-word vocabulary. This was assigned by the project manager.
- The project manager will email the user interface designer her task today.
- The industrial designer will send the user interface designer the vocabulary limits from the chip data sheet.
- The industrial designer will build a non-working foam model with a rounded shape and the stop button in place for the next meeting.
- The marketing expert will draw up evaluation criteria for the final design, based on his research (ease of use with messy hands, look, price).
- The project manager will write up the minutes.

**decisions**
- The timer will use the larger two-hundred-word offline voice chip (5.20 Euro).
- The timer will be splash-proof with a rubber seal, not fully waterproof.
- The timer will keep two microphones.
- The timer will be powered by two AA batteries rather than mains.
- The wake word will be 'Tico'.

**problems**
- The estimated production cost of 12.20 Euro leaves only 30 cents of margin under the 12.50 Euro limit.
- The interaction flow and display layout still need updating for the new chip and wake word, and the spoken confirmations must fit in the two-hundred-word vocabulary.
- The colour of the timer is not decided. The marketing expert announced bright orange, but nobody agreed.
