## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting, welcomed the industrial designer back after his illness, and confirmed that the minutes and the email with the previous meeting's decisions had gone round.
- The marketing expert reported component costs from the supplier price lists: about 10.30 euros in parts without a vibration motor and 11.20 euros with one, excluding the battery, assembly and packaging.
- The user panel's response to a vibration goal alert was mixed, so the team dropped the vibration motor and chose a star icon on the display when a fixed daily goal of 5000 steps is reached.
- The industrial designer presented his battery and clip comparisons, and the team chose a coin-cell battery behind a large twist-off cap and a spring-steel clip.
- The user interface designer presented a mock-up of the three screens with 1.8 centimetre digits, which the marketing expert will test with the user panel.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will take the mock-up to the user panel to check whether it can be read at arm's length.
- The marketing expert will update the cost list for a device with no motor, a coin cell and a steel clip.
- The industrial designer will make a model of the housing with the clip and the twist-off battery cap.
- The user interface designer will finish the screen layout, including the star icon.
- The project manager will send round the minutes.
- Checking what assembly and packaging add to the cost was raised twice, but no owner was assigned.

**decisions**
- The vibration goal alert is dropped. No vibration motor will be included.
- A star icon will appear on the steps screen when the daily goal is reached.
- The daily goal is fixed at 5000 steps and cannot be set by the user.
- The device will use a CR2032 coin-cell battery behind a twist-off cap about 3 centimetres across, with a ridged edge and no screws.
- The device will use a spring-steel clip.

**problems**
- Assembly and packaging costs are unknown, and nobody is assigned to check them.
- The parts alone cost about 10.30 euros, which leaves little margin under the 12.50 euro limit.
- The industrial designer's safety cord loop was left open until the total cost is known.
- The marketing expert again raised the family app, but it remains rejected for this version.
