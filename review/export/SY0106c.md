## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting and confirmed that the minutes of the functional design meeting had been sent and received.
- The marketing expert reported that library networks want a warm, calm look with natural materials and rounded edges rather than a supermarket-till look, and that each network wants its own branding. The team agreed to show the library's logo and colours on the start screen and to support up to six languages, configurable per library.
- The industrial designer presented a pedestal housing concept using the agreed heights, with rounded corners and the key-locked printer panel. The team first agreed on brushed aluminium, but at 1,870 euros in total this was 70 euros over budget, so the team revised the choice to powder-coated mild steel with a birch veneer front panel, bringing the estimate to 1,775 euros.
- The user interface designer presented mock-ups of the checkout flow, including a non-blocking fines banner and a staff maintenance mode with printer guidance. She proposed audio guidance with a headphone jack, which was left open pending a price. She reported that three of the four major catalogue vendors support SIP2 directly. The fourth supports it through an adapter but has not confirmed this in writing, and many libraries do not fill in the SIP2 email field.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will produce the final housing design in powder-coated steel with a birch veneer front panel, with a full cost breakdown and a foam model, for the detailed design meeting.
- The industrial designer will price the audio guidance option (headphone jack and audio board).
- The user interface designer will produce final mock-ups including the library logo bar and the language screen with six languages.
- The user interface designer will chase the fourth catalogue vendor for written confirmation of SIP2 support through an adapter.
- The marketing expert will put together colour options and samples for the powder coat and the veneer.
- The project manager will write up and send the minutes.

**decisions**
- The library's own logo and colours will appear on the start screen in software, rather than each kiosk being painted differently.
- The kiosk will support up to six languages, and each library will configure which ones appear behind the language button.
- The housing will be powder-coated mild steel with a birch veneer front panel. This revises the earlier choice of brushed aluminium in the same meeting, which went over budget.
- Email receipts stay out of the current version, because many libraries do not fill in the SIP2 email field.
- The next meeting, on detailed design, will be held at the same time next Thursday.

**problems**
- The steel and veneer housing leaves only about 25 euros of margin under the 1,800 euro budget.
- Audio guidance with a headphone jack is undecided. Its cost is unknown and an estimated 30 to 40 euros could push the kiosk over budget again.
- The fourth major catalogue vendor's SIP2 support through an adapter is not yet confirmed in writing.
