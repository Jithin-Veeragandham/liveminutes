## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the conceptual design meeting, welcomed back the industrial designer after his illness, and recapped the earlier decisions on the 25 euro price, the 12.50 euro cost limit, two numbered timers and the wake word Kiko.
- The industrial designer confirmed that the speech chip handles the two-timer command set and presented a cost breakdown totalling 10.40 euros per unit with mains power; the team chose mains power over batteries because always-on listening would drain AA batteries in about three weeks.
- With the resulting margin, the team added the small two-digit LED display, which brings the unit cost to 11.30 euros and leaves 1.20 euros under the limit.
- The user interface designer presented the command list and reported that Kiko occasionally triggered on the word 'cocoa' in a laptop test, but the team kept Kiko and will ask the chip supplier to tune it.
- The marketing expert reported that few dedicated voice timers exist, at around 35 to 40 euros, and that smart speakers need wifi and raise privacy complaints, so offline operation will be the main selling point.
- The team agreed on a round, palm-sized, wipeable plastic body whose whole top is one soft rubber button with the display in the middle, and deferred colours to the next meeting.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will ask the chip supplier to tune the Kiko wake word against 'cocoa' and similar words.
- The industrial designer will build a rough model of the timer for the next meeting; this was first asked of the user interface designer.
- The user interface designer will send the completed command list to the team.
- The user interface designer will work out what the display shows with two timers and how the button behaves, and send the display size to the industrial designer.
- The marketing expert will bring three or four colour options to the next meeting.
- The project manager will write up the minutes.

**decisions**
- The timer will be mains powered through a USB-C socket, with a plug adapter included in the box; batteries and a rechargeable pack were rejected.
- A small two-digit LED display (0.90 euros) will be included, bringing the unit cost to 11.30 euros.
- Kiko stays as the wake word despite occasional false triggers on 'cocoa'.
- The product will be marketed as working offline, with no app and no wifi needed.
- The timer will be a round, palm-sized puck in wipeable plastic, with the whole top acting as one soft rubber button and the display in the middle.

**problems**
- Kiko triggered on 'cocoa' in two of about forty attempts in a laptop test; whether the chip supplier can tune this away is not yet known.
- Only 1.20 euros of margin remains under the 12.50 euro limit, so no further components can be added, and the fridge magnet was left out for now.
- Product colours are not yet decided.
