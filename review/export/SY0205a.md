## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting, the team introduced themselves, and she presented the project: a mobile app for a city bike-share operator's roughly four hundred existing docking stations.
- She presented the brief. The app must run on iPhone and Android, let users unlock a bike within thirty seconds of opening the app, and be built on a development budget of 120,000 euros for the app alone. It must also use the operator's branding.
- The team agreed that a map showing live bike and free-slot availability at each station will be the main screen. They discussed availability indicators that do not rely on colour alone.
- The marketing expert identified commuters and tourists as the two main user groups. The team liked the user interface designer's idea of saved favourite docks for commuters.
- The industrial designer will establish which unlock methods the docks support. The competitor app review was moved from the user interface designer to the marketing expert.

**actions**
- The marketing expert will research user requirements for commuters and tourists.
- The marketing expert will do the competitor app review, which was reassigned from the user interface designer, and pass interface findings to him.
- The marketing expert will forward her operator contact to the industrial designer.
- The user interface designer will work on the technical functions of the app.
- The industrial designer will find out which unlock methods the docks support and work on the working design of how the app and dock communicate.
- The project manager will request the operator's brand guide and forward it, and will circulate the minutes and the brief.

**decisions**
- The map with live availability of bikes and free slots will be the app's main screen.
- A screen on the dock itself is out of scope because the docks are already installed.
- Favourite home and work docks for commuters were well received as a feature direction.
- The competitor app review was reassigned from the user interface designer to the marketing expert.

**problems**
- It is unknown which unlock technology (QR, keypad, Bluetooth or NFC) the operator's docks use.
- It is unresolved whether tourists can buy a day pass without a full account, given deposit and theft concerns. This needs checking with the client.
- Availability indicators must work for colour-blind users. Shapes or numbers alongside colour were suggested but not finalised.
- The thirty-second unlock target was considered ambitious.
