## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting, noted that the marketing expert was absent for the day, and had the user interface designer and industrial designer introduce themselves.
- He presented the project, a protective case for the company's existing children's educational tablet, to be sold separately as an accessory and sold internationally, and led a whiteboard warm-up in which each member drew a favourite animal.
- He gave the financial targets: a selling price of 25 euros and a maximum unit production cost of 12.50 euros, excluding packaging.
- The team discussed initial ideas, including the need to survive frequent drops and a carrying handle that could fold into a stand, and agreed on a target age group of four to eight.
- The industrial designer declared EVA foam as the material, but nobody agreed and the project manager deferred the material question. He then assigned preparation tasks, including market research for the absent marketing expert.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will research the working design, including materials and drop protection.
- The user interface designer will study how children interact with the case, including the handle, stand, access to buttons and charging port, and the screen protector.
- The marketing expert, who was absent, will do market research on what parents want in a case and on competitors' products and prices.
- The project manager will email the brief and her task to the marketing expert.
- The project manager will send out the minutes of the meeting.
- The project manager will check with management whether there is a required company colour scheme.

**decisions**
- The target age group for the case is children aged four to eight.

**problems**
- The case material is unresolved. EVA foam was put forward but not agreed, and the decision was postponed until costs are known.
- The 12.50 euro production cost limit is considered tight for a durable children's case.
- It is unknown whether the case must follow a company colour scheme or look.
- The marketing expert has not yet been informed of her assignment.
