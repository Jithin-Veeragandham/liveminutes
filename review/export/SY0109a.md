## Gold: summary (F1)

**abstract**
- The project manager opened the kickoff meeting and the team introduced themselves. He presented the project: a rider-facing app for a city bike-share scheme's docking stations. The pilot covers fourteen docking stations in the city centre. The app has three core functions: find a bike, unlock it at the dock, and return it and pay. It must run on both iOS and Android.
- After a short whiteboard exercise, the team discussed first ideas. These covered how the phone connects to the dock (QR code, Bluetooth or NFC), use outdoors in all weather and at night, one-tap unlocking, and moving payment setup to sign-up. A leaderboard idea from the industrial designer was parked as out of scope for the pilot.
- The next meeting was first set for Wednesday at nine. It was later moved to Friday at ten so that the marketing expert's rider survey results would be available.

**actions**
- The industrial designer will ask the dock supplier how the phone connects to the dock and whether the dock has a screen.
- The user interface designer will produce rough wireframes of the main flows: find, unlock and return.
- The marketing expert will send a quick survey to the pilot waiting list, including one question on the leaderboard idea.
- The marketing expert will review competitor bike-share apps and their user reviews.
- The project manager will send out the minutes, the project brief and the shared folder link.

**decisions**
- The next meeting (functional design) will be on Friday at ten. This replaces the earlier plan of Wednesday at nine.
- Payment setup will happen at sign-up, so that at the dock the rider only needs to unlock.
- Unlocking should be possible in about one tap after opening the app.

**problems**
- The dock hardware is still unknown, including the connection method (QR, Bluetooth or NFC) and whether the dock has a screen. This must be confirmed with the supplier.
- QR-based unlocking may fail in rain, and the screens must be readable outdoors and at night.
- It is unclear whether riders want a leaderboard feature. It was parked pending survey feedback.
